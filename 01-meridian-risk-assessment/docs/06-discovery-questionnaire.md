# Discovery Questionnaire and Evidence Request

Responses should identify the owner, source, effective date, known exceptions, and supporting evidence. `Unknown` is an acceptable answer.

## Governance and business impact

- Who accepts cyber risk and who owns identity, finance fraud, project data, recovery, and third-party risk?
- Which processes cannot tolerate one day, three days, or one week of interruption?
- What financial-loss bands define minor, material, and catastrophic impact?
- Which client, insurance, privacy, or contract requirements apply?
- Provide current policies, incident plans, continuity/recovery procedures, prior assessments, and cyber-insurance control conditions.

## Identity and Microsoft 365

- How many member, guest, privileged, service, shared, and emergency accounts exist?
- Which authentication methods and Conditional Access policies apply to each population?
- Are legacy authentication, user consent, app registrations, and risky sign-ins governed?
- How are administrators separated from daily-use identities and how does the MSP authenticate?
- Provide read-only role, MFA-registration, Conditional Access, sign-in, audit-retention, and application-consent exports.

## SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams, and Procore

- Which repositories contain contracts, bids, employee data, plans, and banking information?
- What external-sharing defaults, anonymous-link settings, expiration rules, and domain restrictions apply?
- Who owns guest and subcontractor access, and how often is it reviewed?
- Are completed projects archived and external users removed?
- Provide site/project lists, administrators, external-user rosters, sharing settings, and a sample completed-project access review.

## Endpoints, mobile, and network

- What is the authoritative inventory and which assets are managed, encrypted, patched, and covered by EDR?
- Who has local administrator rights? How are lost/stolen and job-site devices handled?
- What corporate data can BYOD access, download, or retain? Are MDM/MAM and selective wipe used?
- Are guest Wi-Fi, printers/IoT, user devices, and administrative interfaces separated?
- How is VPN access approved, authenticated, reviewed, logged, and revoked?
- Provide inventory/coverage exports, configuration baselines, sample patch/EDR status, network diagram, firewall/VPN review, and Wi-Fi/VLAN configuration summary.

## Finance and fraud controls

- How are new vendors and bank-detail changes independently verified using trusted contact information?
- Are payment initiation, vendor maintenance, and approval segregated? What are approval thresholds?
- Can approvals occur solely by email? How are urgent executive requests challenged?
- Are bank alerts, positive pay, transfer limits, and reconciliation exceptions used?
- Provide sanitized workflow narratives, role lists, approval matrix, and samples demonstrating operation; do not provide live banking credentials or full account numbers.

## Logging, response, backup, and recovery

- Which identity, email, endpoint, firewall, VPN, Procore, and QuickBooks events are retained and reviewed?
- Who receives alerts after hours, and what is the MSP/insurer/counsel escalation path?
- What are system-specific RTOs/RPOs, backup scope, retention, immutability/isolation, and credential separation?
- When was each critical restore last tested, and were business owners involved?
- Provide logging matrix, escalation contacts, backup architecture, job reports, and restore-test results.

## Third parties and people

- Which vendors hold data, integrate with systems, administer Meridian, or influence payments?
- What security and incident-notification duties are in MSP and critical-vendor agreements?
- How quickly do HR, managers, IT, and MSP complete joiner/mover/leaver changes?
- What awareness training addresses BEC, invoice fraud, QR codes, mobile/job-site work, and reporting?
- Provide vendor inventory, access matrix, relevant contract excerpts, lifecycle tickets/samples, and training metrics.
