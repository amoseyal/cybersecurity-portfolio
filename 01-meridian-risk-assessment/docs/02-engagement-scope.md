# Engagement Scope

## Objective

Identify critical assets, develop defensible risk hypotheses, evaluate available evidence, and prioritize practical improvements for Meridian Build Group’s people, processes, technology, and third-party dependencies.

## In scope

- Governance, policies, roles, incident escalation, and security awareness
- Microsoft Entra ID and Microsoft 365 identity, email, collaboration, and sharing controls
- SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams, and Procore access from Meridian’s side
- QuickBooks Enterprise access and fraud-relevant ACH/wire/vendor-change processes
- Windows endpoints, company mobile devices, and BYOD governance
- Main-office network, corporate/guest Wi-Fi, firewall, VPN, and remote access
- MSP administrative access and controls as they affect Meridian
- Joiner/mover/leaver, privileged access, subcontractor/external access, logging, backup, and recovery
- Business-impact discovery, critical-system recovery needs, and hurricane-related continuity dependencies

## Out of scope

- Active exploitation, penetration testing, denial-of-service, or production attacks
- Phishing or social-engineering simulations
- Job-site physical-security assessments; office security may receive a light-touch observation
- Independent audits of subcontractors, SaaS providers, the MSP’s broader program, or its other clients
- Financial/accounting audit; QuickBooks work is limited to access and fraud-relevant processes
- Formal compliance assessments, certifications, or attestations (including CMMC, SOC 2, or PCI DSS validation)
- Legal or contract review; security-relevant gaps may be flagged for counsel
- Remediation implementation, product deployment, or policy rewriting
- OT/IoT, drones, telematics, site sensors, and embedded equipment unless they connect to Meridian IT; such connections would be documented and separately scoped
- Exhaustive device-by-device testing; endpoint and mobile work uses samples and management evidence
- Development of a comprehensive enterprise BCP/DR plan

## Authorized techniques

Interviews, document review, read-only configuration review, screenshots/exports supplied by authorized personnel, sampling, and non-invasive observation. Any automated scanning would require separate written authorization and rules of engagement.

## Scenario constraints and limitations

- No actual administrator access, interviews, exports, logs, contracts, or configurations exist for this portfolio simulation.
- Technology presence does not imply a security control is enabled.
- Unknowns are not adverse findings; they are discovery requirements.
- Illustrative findings are conditional statements showing how risks would be documented if evidence supported them.

## Stakeholders

Executive sponsor, finance lead, project-operations/Procore owner, HR/operations, internal IT coordinator, MSP representative, and selected business users. Counsel and cyber-insurance contacts participate when escalation criteria require them.
