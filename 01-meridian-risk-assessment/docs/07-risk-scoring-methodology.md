# Risk Scoring Methodology

## Formula

`Inherent risk = Likelihood (1–5) × Impact (1–5)` before considering additional controls. Residual risk is not scored until control design and operating effectiveness are supported by evidence.

## Likelihood

| Score | Descriptor | Evidence-led interpretation |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | Exceptional conditions; strong barriers; little relevant exposure |
| 2 | Unlikely | Possible but requires uncommon conditions or several failures |
| 3 | Possible | Credible scenario with meaningful exposure and mixed/unknown safeguards |
| 4 | Likely | Common threat and accessible path; important safeguards absent or unverified |
| 5 | Almost certain | Repeated/active condition or highly exposed path with little resistance |

## Matrix

| Impact ↓ / Likelihood → | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 — Severe | 5 M | 10 H | 15 H | 20 C | 25 C |
| 4 — Major | 4 L | 8 M | 12 H | 16 H | 20 C |
| 3 — Moderate | 3 L | 6 M | 9 M | 12 H | 15 H |
| 2 — Minor | 2 L | 4 L | 6 M | 8 M | 10 H |
| 1 — Insignificant | 1 L | 2 L | 3 L | 4 L | 5 M |

**Bands:** Low 1–4; Moderate 5–9; High 10–16; Critical 17–25.

## Scoring rules

- Score a defined threat scenario, not an asset in isolation.
- Use the highest credible impact dimension and document rationale.
- Unknown control status does not prove a deficiency; illustrative scores state their simulated preconditions.
- Record existing controls separately and require evidence before applying residual-risk reduction.
- Risk owner—not the assessor—accepts residual risk, with rationale and review date.
- Re-score after remediation validation, material environmental change, or at least annually.
